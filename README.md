# 🐠 AquaLife
### Your TikTok LIVE. A living underwater world.

**Dive in. Make waves. Become a legend of the reef.**

AquaLife transforms a TikTok LIVE into a vibrant, ever-moving aquarium where viewers become fish and every stream becomes a shared underwater adventure. Watch the reef come alive, find your place among the other swimmers, and make your presence count.

This isn't just a backdrop. It's a living arena where the community becomes part of the show.

---

## 🌊 A world that moves with you

🐟 **Become part of the aquarium**  
Viewers appear as their own fish, swimming through a colorful underwater scene with their names visible above them.

💙 **Every like makes waves**  
Send likes to help your fish pick up speed. Stay active, build momentum, and make yourself noticed in the reef.

⚔️ **A little friendly competition**  
Fish meet, clash, and compete in a living ecosystem. Keep an eye on the action and see who rises to the top.

✨ **Grow as you go**  
Activity helps fish become tougher and grow in size. Gifts can bring extra strength and unlock new fish appearances.

🪼 **A reef full of life**  
Enjoy flowing movement, lively sea creatures, underwater scenery, and viewer comments appearing right above the fish they belong to.

🌐 **Everyone has a place**  
Fish enter from different sides of the aquarium and keep swimming naturally. Even when a viewer steps away, their fish can linger briefly before gracefully fading out.

---

## 🏆 Make your mark on the reef

Every viewer adds a little more life to the aquarium. Every like can change the pace. Every encounter creates another moment worth watching.

Will your fish become the star of the stream, or will a new challenger steal the spotlight?

**Join the LIVE. Find your fish. Make waves.**
